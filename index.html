    How to play
    • You get 6 guesses at the monthly change in total nonfarm payrolls, in thousands of jobs.
    • Within ±5K wins. After each guess you see higher or lower, and how close you were: very close is within 50K, close is within 150K.
    • Every miss reveals another figure from the same month.
    • Daily puzzles change at local midnight. Practice draws a random month from 2000 through 2026.
    Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, seasonally adjusted series CES0000000001, LNS14000000, CUSR0000SA0, LNS11300000, CES0500000008, CES3000000001. Figures are current revised estimates, not the first print.
